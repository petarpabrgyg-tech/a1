2. Natural Material Variations and Artisanal Idiosyncrasies

Genuine cambric cloth woven from unbleached flax bast fibers and extra-long staple Egyptian cotton is an organic material subject to inherent natural variations. Slight differences in thread thickness, minor weave slubs, natural color subtleties ranging from warm ecru to oyster white, and textural variations are not manufacturing defects; rather, they serve as definitive hallmarks of authentic artisan hand-shuttle weaving.

Photographic images displayed on our digital gallery are calibrated to reproduce textile colors as faithfully as possible under natural daylight conditions. Nevertheless, slight variations in optical hue, texture, and luminosity will naturally occur across different computer monitors, smart device screens, and ambient lighting environments. Patrons are encouraged to inspect physical cloth swatches during personal salon consultations at our flagship address at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012.

3. Zero Medical, Dermatological, or Chemical Claims

Nothing contained within our discussions regarding natural cellulose fibers, organic wheat starch, or botanical laundering formulations should be interpreted as medical advice, dermatological consultation, or pharmaceutical instruction. While natural flax cambric is traditionally appreciated for its hypoallergenic comfort, breathable nature, and smooth skin hand, our garments are not engineered to mitigate, prevent, or address dermatological disorders.

Individuals with severe contact allergies, specific sensitivity to agricultural starches, or specialized dermatological requirements should consult licensed medical healthcare practitioners before wearing heavily starched collars or experimenting with traditional starch solutions.

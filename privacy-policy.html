2. Legitimate Purposes of Information Processing

Our processing of your personal information is governed rigorously by principles of necessity, absolute transparency, and lawful operational purpose. 3. Absolute Data Shielding and Third-Party Non-Disclosure

Under no circumstances does Cambric Panache sell, lease, commercialize, or barter your personal identifiable information to third-party commercial marketing brokerages. Your data remains protected within our encrypted institutional enclave, accessible only to designated atelier staff members who require specific measurements to cut and hand-sew your bespoke commissions. Any external service providers we utilize, such as our payment clearinghouses, operate under binding non-disclosure agreements and institutional audits.

We disclose client information solely when strictly compelled by sovereign judicial decrees, valid law enforcement subpoenas, or applicable regulatory mandates issued within the United States. Should our organizational structure ever undergo an institutional reorganization or merger, patron measurement archives will transition exclusively to a successor entity bound by the identical strict confidentiality covenants set forth within this institutional privacy document without exception.

4. Data Security Architecture and Physical Safe-Keeping

We employ multi-tiered technical, administrative, and physical safeguards engineered to shield your personal details against unauthorized interception, unlawful disclosure, or accidental destruction. All electronic transmissions containing sensitive patron inputs are encrypted using current Transport Layer Security protocols before traversing public networks. Digital customer files are stored within firewalled, encrypted databases featuring continuous intrusion detection and monitoring across our infrastructure.
